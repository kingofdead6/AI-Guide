Two views of Ax\mathbf{Ax}Ax that you should keep in mind at all times:

  • Row view: entry iii is the dot product of row iii with x\mathbf{x}x.
  • Column view: Ax=∑jxj aj\mathbf{Ax}=\sum_j x_j\,\mathbf{a}_jAx=∑j​xj​aj​, a linear combination of the columns. So Ax=b\mathbf{Ax}=\mathbf{b}Ax=b is solvable exactly when b\mathbf{b}b lies in the column space.

Essential facts

  • Not commutative: AB≠BA\mathbf{AB}\ne\mathbf{BA}AB=BA in general. Associative: (AB)C=A(BC)(\mathbf{AB})\mathbf{C}=\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{BC})(AB)C=A(BC).
  • Transpose: (AB)⊤=B⊤A⊤(\mathbf{AB})^\top=\mathbf{B}^\top\mathbf{A}^\top(AB)⊤=B⊤A⊤. Inverse: (AB)−1=B−1A−1(\mathbf{AB})^{-1}=\mathbf{B}^{-1}\mathbf{A}^{-1}(AB)−1=B−1A−1.
  • Rank = dimension of the column space = number of independent columns. A square matrix is invertible   ⟺  \iff⟺ full rank   ⟺  det⁡A≠0\iff \det\mathbf{A}\ne0⟺detA=0.
  • ∣det⁡A∣|\det\mathbf{A}|∣detA∣ is the factor by which A\mathbf{A}A scales volumes. Zero determinant means some direction is squashed flat.
  • Trace tr⁡(A)=∑iAii\operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{A})=\sum_i A_{ii}tr(A)=∑i​Aii​, and tr⁡(AB)=tr⁡(BA)\operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{AB})=\operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{BA})tr(AB)=tr(BA).
