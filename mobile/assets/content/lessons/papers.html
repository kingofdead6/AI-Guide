The three-pass method

  1. 5 minutes: title, abstract, figures, conclusion. What is the claim? Is it relevant?
  2. 1 hour: the method and main results. Note the baseline, datasets and the key equation or diagram.
  3. Deep: re-derive the math and re-implement the core idea in a small notebook. This is where real understanding happens.

Questions to ask every paper

What exactly is new? What are the baselines, and are they tuned fairly? Is the improvement larger than seed-to-seed variance? What does it cost (compute, data)? What is not evaluated? Would this matter for a real product?
