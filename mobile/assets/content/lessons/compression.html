Knowledge distillation

Train a small student to match a large teacher's soft output distribution, which carries "dark knowledge" (a 2 that looks a bit like a 7). With temperature TTT:

L=α T2 DKL ⁣(softmax⁡(zt/T) ∥ softmax⁡(zs/T))+(1−α) CE(y,zs)\mathcal L=\alpha\,T^2\,D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\Big(\operatorname{softmax}(\mathbf z_t/T)\,\big\|\,\operatorname{softmax}(\mathbf z_s/T)\Big)+(1-\alpha)\,\text{CE}(y,\mathbf z_s)L=αT2DKL​(softmax(zt​/T)​softmax(zs​/T))+(1−α)CE(y,zs​)

The T2T^2T2 factor keeps gradient magnitudes comparable across temperatures. For LLMs, distillation is often done on sequences: the student is fine-tuned on text the teacher generates.

Pruning

Unstructured pruning zeros individual small-magnitude weights (high sparsity possible, but speedups need sparse kernels). Structured pruning removes whole channels, heads or layers, giving real speedups on ordinary hardware. Prune gradually and fine-tune to recover. (The "lottery ticket" result showed sparse subnetworks that train well from their original initialization exist.)

Quantization

Post-training quantization (PTQ) converts a trained model's weights (and possibly activations) to int8/int4 using a small calibration set. Quantization-aware training (QAT) simulates rounding during training so the model adapts; it recovers more accuracy at very low bit-widths. Per-channel or per-group scales and handling activation outliers are what make 4-bit LLMs work (details in Fine-Tuning, LoRA & Quantization).

Deployment stack

Export with ONNX or torch.export, then compile with an optimizing runtime (ONNX Runtime, TensorRT, OpenVINO, Core ML, TFLite / LiteRT for mobile). Always measure on the target hardware: fewer FLOPs does not automatically mean lower latency.

def distill_loss(student_logits, teacher_logits, labels, T=4.0, alpha=0.7):
    soft = F.kl_div(F.log_softmax(student_logits / T, -1), F.softmax(teacher_logits / T, -1),
                    reduction="batchmean") * T * T
    hard = F.cross_entropy(student_logits, labels)
    return alpha * soft + (1 - alpha) * hard
