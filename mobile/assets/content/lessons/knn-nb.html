k-NN

Predict by majority vote (or average) of the kkk closest training points. Small kkk: low bias, high variance. Large kkk: smoother. It needs feature scaling, suffers from the curse of dimensionality, and prediction costs O(nd)O(nd)O(nd) per query unless you use an index (the same ANN indexes as vector databases, Embeddings & Vector Search).
