Definitions

A vector x∈Rn\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^nx∈Rn is an ordered list of nnn real numbers. Vectors add component-wise and scale by scalars. Any expression ∑icivi\sum_i c_i\mathbf{v}_i∑i​ci​vi​ is a linear combination; the set of all such combinations is the span. Vectors are linearly independent if none of them is a combination of the others. A basis is an independent set that spans the space, and its size is the dimension.

Where ML uses itFeature vectors and embeddings. Cosine similarity is how RAG finds relevant documents (RAG, Agents & LLMOps). Every neuron computes a dot product w⊤x\mathbf{w}^\top\mathbf{x}w⊤x. L2 and L1 norms become weight decay and sparsity penalties (Linear Regression, Ridge & Lasso). Attention scores are dot products between queries and keys (Attention & the Transformer).
