One-hot vectors are huge and every pair is orthogonal ("cat" is as far from "kitten" as from "carburetor"). Embeddings map each word to vw∈Rd\mathbf v_w\in\mathbb R^{d}vw​∈Rd with ddd around 100–300.

The denominator sums over the whole vocabulary, which is too expensive. Negative sampling turns it into binary classification: real pairs versus kkk random "noise" words:

L=−log⁡σ(uo⊤vc)−∑j=1klog⁡σ(−unj⊤vc)\mathcal L=-\log\sigma(\mathbf u_o^\top\mathbf v_c)-\sum_{j=1}^{k}\log\sigma(-\mathbf u_{n_j}^\top\mathbf v_c)L=−logσ(uo⊤​vc​)−j=1∑k​logσ(−unj​⊤​vc​)

GloVe instead fits embeddings to global co-occurrence statistics: vi⊤uj+bi+bj≈log⁡Xij\mathbf v_i^\top\mathbf u_j+b_i+b_j\approx\log X_{ij}vi⊤​uj​+bi​+bj​≈logXij​.
