Pipeline

Normalize (lowercase, Unicode normalization, and for Arabic, normalizing letter variants and removing diacritics where appropriate) → tokenize → optionally remove stopwords and stem or lemmatize → vectorize.

Bag of words and TF-IDF

A document becomes a vector of word counts (word order lost). TF-IDF reweights so words frequent in this document but rare across the corpus count most:

tfidf(t,d)=tf(t,d)⋅log⁡Ndf(t)\text{tfidf}(t,d)=\text{tf}(t,d)\cdot\log\frac{N}{\text{df}(t)}tfidf(t,d)=tf(t,d)⋅logdf(t)N​

Counts are sparse, so smoothing is required (add-one, Kneser–Ney). Neural language models replaced counts with learned representations that generalize to unseen contexts, but perplexity remains the standard intrinsic metric.

Classic tasks

Text classification (sentiment, topic, spam), named-entity recognition, part-of-speech tagging, question answering, summarization, translation. Today most are solved by fine-tuning pretrained Transformers (6.4) or prompting LLMs (Large Language Models).
