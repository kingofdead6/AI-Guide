Why the gradient is the direction of steepest ascent

The rate of change of fff along a unit direction u\mathbf{u}u is the directional derivative Duf=∇f⊤uD_{\mathbf{u}}f=\nabla f^\top\mathbf{u}Du​f=∇f⊤u. By Cauchy–Schwarz, ∇f⊤u≤∥∇f∥\nabla f^\top\mathbf{u}\le\|\nabla f\|∇f⊤u≤∥∇f∥, with equality when u=∇f/∥∇f∥\mathbf{u}=\nabla f/\|\nabla f\|u=∇f/∥∇f∥. So ∇f\nabla f∇f points uphill fastest and −∇f-\nabla f−∇f points downhill fastest. That one fact is the basis of gradient descent.
