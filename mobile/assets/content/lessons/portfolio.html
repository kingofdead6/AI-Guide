What makes a project stand out

  • A real problem and real data, ideally one that matters locally (Arabic/Darija text, Algerian products or services, regional languages): differentiated and hard to copy.
  • A baseline and an evaluation: show what you beat and by how much, with a proper test set.
  • Deployment: a live demo or API with a README that explains how to run it.
  • A write-up: the problem, decisions, what failed, what you'd do next. This is what interviewers read.
  • Engineering quality: tests, configs, clean structure, reproducible training.

Getting noticed

Keep GitHub tidy with pinned projects. Write about your work. Contribute to open source (docs and small fixes count, and they build relationships). Kaggle and hackathons give credible signals. Tailor your CV: impact first ("reduced latency 40%", "improved F1 from 0.71 to 0.83"), the stack second. Apply broadly, including remote roles and research-engineering positions, and ask for referrals from people who know your work.
