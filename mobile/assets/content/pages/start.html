AI looks like a zoo of unrelated techniques. It isn't. Almost everything reduces to a few ideas: represent data as vectors, define a probabilistic model or a loss, and minimize that loss with gradients.

How the academy is organized

There are sixteen courses in five tracks. Each course is a sequence of lessons; each lesson ends with a short graded quiz; each course ends with a final exam and a certificate.

How to study each lesson

  1. Read the intuition first, then the equations. For every symbol ask what it means and what shape it has.
  2. Re-derive on paper. Close the page and reproduce the key derivation. If you can't, you don't own it yet.
  3. Play with the boxes marked "Try it". Change one setting at a time and predict what will happen before you look.
  4. Run and modify the code. Copy any code block with its Copy button, change one thing, predict the result, then check.
  5. Try the practice problems inside the lesson before opening their solutions.
  6. Pass the quiz. Answer each question, press Check, and read the explanation whether you were right or wrong. You need 60% to complete the lesson, and you can retry as often as you like.
  7. Do the code exercises when a lesson has them. They run real Python in your browser and test your function automatically.

Exams and certificates

  • A course's final exam opens when every lesson in it is complete. It draws up to 20 questions from the whole course, including integrative questions that never appear in lesson quizzes.
  • You need 70% to pass. Retakes are unlimited, each with a fresh draw of questions, and your best score counts.
  • Passing the exam with every lesson complete earns the course certificate. Earning all sixteen earns the program certificate.
  • Certificates record self-paced completion in this academy. They are not academic degrees.

Where your progress is saved

Everything is saved automatically in this browser: completed lessons, quiz answers, exam results, code, project milestones and certificates. Nothing is sent to a server. To move to another device or protect against clearing your browser data, download a backup from My progress and restore it on the other device.
